About me

Building tools that simplify the work of public service

"The best technology is the kind that gets out of the way and lets people focus on what truly matters."

I'm Guan, an Engineer in the Singapore Public Service. My work sits at the intersection of technology and people — I build and develop low-code and no-code solutions that help public officers work more efficiently, without needing to write a single line of code.

I believe that great internal tools should feel effortless. Whether it's automating a repetitive workflow, digitising a paper process, or giving officers a dashboard that actually makes sense — I focus on creating solutions that people genuinely want to use.

I am proficient in Python, Tableau, Microsoft Power Platform and UiPath RPA.

Based in Singapore, I'm passionate about leveraging modern platforms to accelerate how the public sector delivers value to citizens.

Currently, I have an interest to learn to build pro-code solutions with the help of AI. I hope I can bring more values to the humankind in times to come.
